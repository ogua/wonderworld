For an international-standard school website, focus on these essentials:

1. *Homepage*

   * School name, logo, motto and clear introductory statement
   * Classes offered, location and contact details
   * “Apply Now,” “Book a Tour” and “Contact Us” buttons
   * High-quality photographs of real school activities

2. *About the School*

   * School history
   * Vision, mission and core values
   * Director or Head of School’s welcome message
   * What makes the school different

3. *Curriculum*

   * Curriculum followed, including OIC
   * Subjects and learning approach
   * Early Years, Primary and other available levels
   * Assessment and reporting methods
   * Co-curricular activities such as robotics, coding, music, ballet, karate and abacus

4. *Accreditation and Licensing*

   * NaSIA licence information
   * Exact Oxford International Curriculum status
   * Approved partnerships and affiliations
   * Certificates or verification details

Use the precise official wording. Do not describe the school as “Oxford accredited” unless that specific accreditation has formally been granted.

5. *Admissions*

   * Admission process
   * Entry ages and available classes
   * Required documents
   * School fees or instructions for obtaining the fee schedule
   * Application form and school-tour booking

6. *Leadership*

   * Director or Head of School
   * Board or governance structure
   * Academic leadership team
   * Short professional profiles

7. *School Life*

   * School hours
   * Uniforms
   * Meals, transport and after-school care
   * Clubs, competitions, trips and events

8. *Safeguarding and Learner Support*

   * Child protection statement
   * Safeguarding contact
   * Anti-bullying procedures
   * Special educational needs and learner well-being support

9. * WWIS Parent Information*

   * School calendar
   * Parent handbook
   * Attendance, behaviour and fee policies
   * Communication and complaints procedures

10. *Contact Page*

* Full address and map
* Telephone and WhatsApp numbers
* Email address
* Social-media links
* Contact form
